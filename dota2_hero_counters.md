# Dota 2 Complete Hero & Item Counter Guide (All 127 Heroes)

A comprehensive, alphabetical reference of every hero in **Dota 2** (all 127 heroes including **Ringmaster**, **Kez**, and **Largo**), detailing each hero's core vulnerability, **counter heroes (weak against)**, **counter items**, and **heroes countered (strong against)** with mechanical explanations.

> [!TIP]
> **Universal Counter Mechanics Quick Reference**
> - **Break** (*Silver Edge, Viper's Nethertoxin, Hoodwink's Scepter, Shadow Demon's Demonic Cleanse*): Disables passive abilities (e.g., Bristleback, Spectre's Dispersion, Phantom Assassin's Blur, Huskar's Berserker's Blood, Tidehunter's Kraken Shell).
> - **Heal / Regeneration Reduction** (*Spirit Vessel, Shiva's Guard, Eye of Skadi, Orb of Corrosion, Ancient Apparition's Ice Blast, Doom*): Shuts down high-sustain and lifesteal cores (e.g., Huskar, Alchemist, Morphling, Necrophos, Io, Mundo-style tanks).
> - **Nullifier** (*Continuous Basic Dispel*): Strips positive buffs and prevents enemies from using Ghost Scepter, Ethereal Blade, Force Staff, Glimmer Cape, Eul's Scepter, Aeon Disk, or kit buffs like Windrun and Magnetic Flux.
> - **Mana Burn & Silence** (*Diffusal Blade, Disperser, Orchid Malevolence, Bloodthorn, Anti-Mage, Nyx Assassin*): Cripples low-cooldown mobility spirits (Storm, Ember, Void, Puck) and high-cost spellcasters (Medusa, Wraith King, Timber, Largo).

## A

### Abaddon (Universal)
- **Key Weakness:** Abaddon relies heavily on Borrowed Time's damage-to-healing conversion and Aphotic Shield's strong dispel to survive and protect allies, making him vulnerable to heal prevention, Break mechanics combined with silences, and forced banish/cyclone effects that wait out his ultimate.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Ancient Apparition:** Ice Blast completely blocks all healing and regeneration, rendering Mist Coil's heal and Borrowed Time's damage-to-heal conversion useless while executing Abaddon once he drops below the shatter threshold.
  - **Outworld Destroyer:** Astral Imprisonment banishes Abaddon during Borrowed Time so the duration ticks down harmlessly without him receiving any healing, and Sanity's Eclipse deals massive burst damage that can potentially kill him from above the 400 HP passive trigger threshold.
  - **Shadow Demon:** Disruption hides Abaddon for the majority of Borrowed Time's duration while creating illusions that carry Curse of Avernus against his own team, and Demonic Purge continuously dispels Aphotic Shield.
  - **Doom:** Doom applies mute and prevents health regeneration/healing, stopping Abaddon from manually casting Borrowed Time or healing during its active window.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Eul's Scepter of Divinity:** Cyclones Abaddon into the air for 2.5 seconds after Borrowed Time triggers, wasting nearly half of his ultimate and preventing him from hitting targets or dispelling allies.
  - **Silver Edge:** Applies Break so Borrowed Time cannot passively trigger at the 400 HP threshold, allowing teams to burst or silence Abaddon to death before he can manually activate it.
  - **Shiva's Guard:** Reduces Abaddon's healing and regeneration while slowing his attack and movement speed, limiting his ability to stack Curse of Avernus.
  - **Nullifier:** Continuously dispels Aphotic Shield on Abaddon or his allies before the shield can absorb full damage and burst.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**
  - **Bane:** Aphotic Shield applies a strong dispel that immediately removes Nightmare, Enfeeble, and even breaks Fiend's Grip on an allied target from safe range.
  - **Batrider:** Aphotic Shield's low-cooldown strong dispel clears Sticky Napalm stacks in lane and instantly frees an allied hero caught in Flaming Lasso.
  - **Mirana:** Aphotic Shield immediately wakes allies hit by even a maximum-duration Sacred Arrow, completely negating Mirana's pickoff setup.
  - **Bloodseeker:** Borrowed Time converts all Rupture movement damage into healing, allowing Abaddon to sprint across the map while Ruptured to heal back to 100% HP.

### Alchemist (Strength)
- **Key Weakness:** Alchemist depends on Chemical Rage's massive health regeneration and Greevil's Greed gold acceleration to stay ahead in net worth, leaving him vulnerable to early jungle pressure, armor reduction, and anti-healing effects.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Ancient Apparition:** Ice Blast completely negates Chemical Rage's extreme health regeneration, turning Alchemist into a low-effective-HP target during teamfights.
  - **Slardar:** Corrosive Haze shreds Alchemist's naturally low base armor and provides true sight, allowing physical carries to melt him faster than Chemical Rage can regenerate.
  - **Ursa:** Fury Swipes stack rapidly on Alchemist's melee frame, dealing escalating physical damage that easily outpaces Chemical Rage regeneration in prolonged manfights.
  - **Lifestealer:** Feast deals percentage-based damage and heals Lifestealer based on Alchemist's high HP pool during Chemical Rage, allowing Lifestealer to out-trade him directly.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Spirit Vessel:** Soul Release slashes Chemical Rage's health regeneration by a huge percentage while dealing max-HP percentage damage per second.
  - **Eye of Skadi:** Reduces all health regeneration and healing on Alchemist while slowing his movement and attack speed through debuff immunity, allowing ranged cores to kite him.
  - **Shiva's Guard:** Provides an aura that reduces Alchemist's Chemical Rage regeneration, slows his attack speed, and increases the magic damage he takes.
  - **Assault Cuirass:** Grants team-wide armor against Acid Spray's armor corruption and Alchemist's high physical attack speed while reducing Alchemist's own armor.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**
  - **Nature's Prophet:** Acid Spray and Battle Fury/Radiance timing allow Alchemist to farm Treants rapidly for bonus Greevil's Greed gold, turning Nature's Prophet's summons into a massive net-worth boost.
  - **Broodmother:** Spiderlings and Spiderites feed enormous Greevil's Greed bonus gold into Acid Spray, accelerating Alchemist's six-slotted timing faster than Broodmother can close out the game.
  - **Undying:** Greevil's Greed triggers on Undying's Tombstone zombies, healing Alchemist via lifesteal/cleave while generating thousands of extra gold during teamfights.
  - **Spectre:** Alchemist hits an overwhelming 15-to-25-minute item timing and gifts Aghanim's Scepters to his team long before Spectre can farm the items needed to contest high ground.

### Ancient Apparition (Intelligence)
- **Key Weakness:** Ancient Apparition is an immobile, fragile backline support with no built-in escape mechanism and a delayed-trigger stun in Cold Feet, making him easy prey for high-mobility gap-closers and dispels.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Anti-Mage:** Blink easily closes the gap onto Ancient Apparition in the backlines and allows Anti-Mage to step out of Cold Feet's tether radius before the stun triggers, while Counterspell passively reduces AA's high magic output.
  - **Phantom Assassin:** Phantom Strike jumps directly onto Ancient Apparition and bursts his low armor pool in seconds before Chilling Touch or Cold Feet can save him.
  - **Weaver:** Shukuchi grants max movement speed and invisibility to break Cold Feet's tether effortlessly, and Time Lapse completely removes Ice Blast's frostbite curse if Weaver is not shattered immediately.
  - **Oracle:** Fate's Edict grants 100% magic damage resistance to negate Ice Blast's shatter damage and Chilling Touch harassment, while False Promise delays all damage until Ice Blast's debuff expires.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Force Staff:** Pushes yourself or an ally 600 units away to immediately break the Cold Feet tether before the 4-second freeze activates.
  - **Black King Bar:** Grants debuff immunity and high magic resistance, preventing Cold Feet and Ice Vortex from applying and blocking magic damage from Chilling Touch and Ice Blast.
  - **Pipe of Insight:** Barrier absorbs a large portion of Ice Blast's impact and damage-over-time across the entire team, preventing allies from dropping below the shatter threshold.
  - **Blink Dagger:** Allows initiators to jump straight into the backlines to burst Ancient Apparition before he can position an effective long-range Ice Blast.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**
  - **Huskar:** Ice Blast completely disables Berserker's Blood health regeneration and Life Break/lifesteal healing, causing Huskar to shatter quickly because he operates at low HP.
  - **Alchemist:** Ice Blast negates Chemical Rage's extreme health regeneration, stripping Alchemist of his main survivability mechanism in teamfights.
  - **Morphling:** Ice Blast prevents Morphling from gaining effective HP when shifting attributes to Strength via Attribute Shift and blocks Satanic/Morbid Mask lifesteal.
  - **Necrophos:** Ice Blast stops Ghost Shroud's amplified healing, Death Pulse heals, and Sadist regeneration, turning Necrophos into an easy burst target.

### Anti-Mage (Agility)
- **Key Weakness:** Anti-Mage is weak in the early laning and mid-game stages before farming Battle Fury and Manta Style, and he struggles against instant lockdowns, non-targeted disables that bypass Counterspell, and pure physical burst.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Legion Commander:** Duel locks Anti-Mage in place through debuff immunity with Blade Mail active, forcing him to attack himself to death with Mana Break and preventing him from using Blink.
  - **Axe:** Berserker's Call pierces debuff immunity and bypasses Counterspell because it is a non-targeted AoE taunt, holding Anti-Mage down while Counter Helix deals pure damage.
  - **Phantom Assassin:** Stifling Dagger and Phantom Strike dish out overwhelming physical burst damage that completely ignores Counterspell's passive magic resistance, and Blur allows her to farm and ambush him undetectable on the minimap.
  - **Meepo:** Earthbind chains root Anti-Mage to disable Blink for extended periods, and Meepo's early power timing overwhelms Anti-Mage's jungle before he comes online.
  - **Huskar:** Huskar's innate Blood Magic gives him zero max mana, completely nullifying Mana Break's bonus damage and Mana Void, while his early Armlet timing and Aghanim's Scepter Life Break taunt lock down and overrun Anti-Mage before he can farm.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Orchid Malevolence / Bloodthorn:** Silences Anti-Mage instantly from fog before he can react with Counterspell or Blink, forcing him to rush Manta Style or Black King Bar to survive ganks.
  - **Scythe of Vyse:** Instant hex with zero cast-point animation gives Anti-Mage virtually no time to activate Counterspell's reflection shell unless pre-cast.
  - **Ghost Scepter:** Prevents Anti-Mage and his Manta Style illusions from attacking the user, stopping him from burning mana and building up a lethal Mana Void on supports.
  - **Silver Edge:** Breaks Mana Break and Counterspell's passive magic resistance while providing surprise initiation out of invisibility.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**
  - **Medusa:** Mana Break shreds Medusa's Mana Shield rapidly to strip her primary durability, and Mana Void deals catastrophic AoE damage based on her massive missing mana pool.
  - **Storm Spirit:** Storm Spirit expends huge amounts of mana with Ball Lightning, setting himself up for an instant one-shot execution from Mana Void that also obliterates nearby allies.
  - **Pugna:** Pugna has the highest intelligence growth in the game coupled with low armor, making him an easy target to jump with Blink and a walking bomb for Mana Void.
  - **Zeus:** Counterspell passively blocks a huge portion of Zeus's magical damage and reflects Lightning Bolt, while Zeus's deep mana pool fuels lethal Mana Voids.

### Arc Warden (Universal)
- **Key Weakness:** Arc Warden has zero mobility skills and struggles when enemies close the gap directly on the real hero, bypassing Magnetic Field's evasion and disabling his ability to safely micro Tempest Double from afar.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Broodmother:** Insatiable Hunger and a swarm of Spiderlings overrun Arc Warden early, automatically soaking and triggering Flux and Spark Wraiths so neither spell works effectively.
  - **Phantom Lancer:** Juxtapose illusions constantly trigger Spark Wraiths and mute Flux's single-target requirement, while Phantom Rush closes the gap on Arc Warden relentlessly.
  - **Anti-Mage:** Blink jumps straight past Spark Wraith traps onto the main Arc Warden inside Magnetic Field, and Mana Void punishes Arc Warden's large intelligence and mana pool.
  - **Lycan:** Shapeshift grants unslowable haste to Lycan and all his summons, running past Flux slows and tearing down the real Arc Warden before he can disengage.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Monkey King Bar:** Pierce procs ignore Magnetic Field's 100% evasion bonus from outside or inside the bubble, allowing ranged and melee cores to hit Arc Warden reliably.
  - **Blink Dagger:** Enables melee cores and initiators to close the gap directly inside Magnetic Field, nullifying its evasion mechanic and punishing Arc Warden's poor mobility.
  - **Black King Bar:** Negates Flux's slow and damage-over-time as well as Spark Wraith's magical burst and slow during crucial teamfight engagements.
  - **Manta Style:** Creates illusions to stand next to the hero to instantly mute Flux, and the activation dispels Flux if already applied.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**
  - **Drow Ranger:** Magnetic Field grants 100% evasion against ranged attacks from outside the bubble, forcing Drow Ranger to either buy an early Monkey King Bar or walk into perilous close range.
  - **Sniper:** Sniper lacks mobility to enter Magnetic Field and cannot damage buildings or heroes inside the bubble with Take Aim right-clicks until he purchases Monkey King Bar.
  - **Terrorblade:** Tempest Double allows Arc Warden to kite Terrorblade's immobile Metamorphosis from long range with Flux and Spark Wraiths while denying physical right-click damage via Magnetic Field.
  - **Outworld Destroyer:** Arc Warden outranges Outworld Destroyer's Arcane Orb and Astral Imprisonment, using Tempest Double to silence, hex, and burst OD without risking the real hero.

### Axe (Strength)
- **Key Weakness:** Axe relies on blinking into grouped enemies and surviving through high armor during Berserker's Call, making him vulnerable to kiting, status resistance, banish saves, and percentage-based or magical damage outside of Call.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Ursa:** Fury Swipes punish Axe in lane and teamfights, and Enrage provides huge damage reduction and status resistance that drastically shortens Berserker's Call duration.
  - **Lifestealer:** Feast heals Lifestealer and deals damage based on Axe's high strength and HP pool, while Rage grants magic immunity and sustains through Counter Helix in lane.
  - **Outworld Destroyer:** Astral Imprisonment banishes either Axe or the primary target caught in Berserker's Call, completely wasting Call's duration and denying Culling Blade resets.
  - **Venomancer:** Venomous Gale and Poison Sting continuously cancel Axe's Blink Dagger from long range and kite him effortlessly whenever Berserker's Call is on cooldown.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Eul's Scepter of Divinity / Wind Waker:** Cyclones Axe immediately after he blinks in and casts Berserker's Call, wasting the entire taunt duration in the air so he cannot trigger Counter Helix.
  - **Silver Edge:** Applies Break to disable Counter Helix completely, reducing Axe's damage output and lane/teamfight threat to near zero outside of his taunt.
  - **Aeon Disk:** Triggers Combo Breaker when Axe initiates with Berserker's Call and Blade Mail, granting strong dispel and 0 damage taken so Culling Blade cannot execute.
  - **Ethereal Blade:** Saves an ally caught in Berserker's Call by turning them ethereal so they cannot be damaged by physical attacks, or bursts Axe with magic damage once Call's bonus armor ends.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**
  - **Dazzle:** Culling Blade pierces Shallow Grave and instantly executes targets below the HP threshold, completely invalidating Dazzle's core defensive spell.
  - **Phantom Lancer:** Berserker's Call locks Phantom Lancer and all of his Juxtapose illusions into attacking Axe simultaneously, triggering nonstop pure-damage Counter Helix spins that wipe out the illusion army.
  - **Huskar:** Berserker's Call forces Huskar's high attack speed onto Axe during Blade Mail, and Culling Blade executes Huskar at the low health threshold where Berserker's Blood is strongest.
  - **Anti-Mage:** Berserker's Call is a non-targeted AoE disable that pierces debuff immunity and cannot be reflected by Counterspell, holding Anti-Mage down while Counter Helix and Blade Mail shred him.

## B

### Bane (Universal)
- **Key Weakness:** Bane's strongest disables (Fiend's Grip and Nightmare) are single-target and either channeled or easily transferred, making him highly vulnerable to illusions, basic dispels, Lotus Orb reflections, and long-range interrupts.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Abaddon:** Aphotic Shield applies a strong dispel that instantly removes Nightmare, Enfeeble, or even Fiend's Grip from an allied target.
  - **Legion Commander:** Press the Attack provides a low-cooldown strong dispel that immediately wakes allies from Nightmare or breaks them out of Fiend's Grip.
  - **Silencer:** Global Silence interrupts Fiend's Grip from anywhere on the map regardless of Bane's positioning, Glimmer Cape invisibility, or Black King Bar.
  - **Phantom Lancer:** Doppelganger and Juxtapose illusions overwhelm Bane's strictly single-target kit, and illusions can attack Nightmared allies to transfer the sleep onto themselves.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Linken's Sphere:** Blocks Brain Sap, Enfeeble, Nightmare, or Fiend's Grip on cast, forcing Bane to waste a long-cast-point spell before he can lock down his target.
  - **Lotus Orb:** Echo Shell reflects Fiend's Grip or Nightmare right back onto Bane, instantly canceling his channeled ultimate as soon as he casts it.
  - **Glimmer Cape:** Wraps the Fiend's Grip victim in invisibility and a magic barrier, absorbing a large portion of the pure/magical drain and protecting them from follow-up hits.
  - **Manta Style:** Dispels Enfeeble and spawns illusions that can either interrupt Bane or attack a Nightmared teammate to take the Nightmare debuff off the real hero.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**
  - **Lifestealer:** Fiend's Grip pierces Rage's debuff immunity and holds Lifestealer in place for up to 6+ seconds while draining his mana and health, completely shutting down his Rage window.
  - **Phantom Assassin:** Brain Sap deals pure damage that ignores Blur's evasion, Enfeeble cuts her attack damage and cast range, and Fiend's Grip locks her down through Black King Bar.
  - **Faceless Void:** Nightmare and Fiend's Grip disable Faceless Void from outside Chronosphere (or pierce his BKB inside it), wasting his entire ultimate duration so he cannot get a kill.
  - **Troll Warlord:** Fiend's Grip pierces BKB and holds Troll Warlord stationary during Battle Trance, preventing him from attacking or lifestealing until his ultimate expires.

### Batrider (Universal)
- **Key Weakness:** Batrider depends on stacking Sticky Napalm in lane and dragging a key target with Flaming Lasso, so cheap dispels, strong dispels on allies, and status resistance severely neuter his impact.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Abaddon:** Aphotic Shield's strong dispel instantly breaks Flaming Lasso on an ally and clears all Sticky Napalm stacks during the laning phase.
  - **Oracle:** Fortune's End dispels Sticky Napalm stacks and Flamebreak slows, while False Promise applies a strong dispel on cast to immediately free a Lassoed teammate.
  - **Vengeful Spirit:** Nether Swap instantly pulls a Lassoed ally out of enemy reach or swaps Batrider deep into the defending team to break his positioning.
  - **Nyx Assassin:** Spiked Carapace stuns Batrider effortlessly whenever he walks over with Firefly or throws Flamebreak, ruining his Blink-Lasso initiation.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Magic Wand:** Sticky Napalm spam rapidly fills up 20 charges in lane, providing burst heal and mana that allows laners to survive Batrider's dive attempts.
  - **Linken's Sphere:** Blocks Flaming Lasso on contact, forcing Batrider to purchase and cast a secondary single-target item during his narrow Blink initiation window.
  - **Lotus Orb:** Dispels Sticky Napalm stacks when cast and reflects Flaming Lasso onto Batrider if active when he initiates, tethering both heroes in place.
  - **Sange and Yasha:** Grants passive status resistance that significantly reduces Flaming Lasso's duration and limits how far Batrider can drag his victim.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**
  - **Meepo:** Sticky Napalm and Firefly turn slowing turn-rates and AoE ground burn into a nightmare for all Meepo clones simultaneously, and Flaming Lasso drags a single clone far away to die.
  - **Monkey King:** Firefly and Flamebreak easily destroy the trees Monkey King perches on with Tree Dance, triggering a 4-second primal stun and ruining Wukong's Command positioning.
  - **Templar Assassin:** Firefly's rapid 0.5-second damage-over-time ticks burn through all charges of Refraction in moments and reveal Templar Assassin's position if she uses Meld.
  - **Chen:** Sticky Napalm and Firefly slow and melt Chen's entire creep army at once while Flamebreak knocks his grouped summons out of position.

### Beastmaster (Universal)
- **Key Weakness:** Beastmaster relies on his summoned Boars and Hawk (or Wild Axes amp) for lane dominance and Primal Roar for single-target lockdown, making him vulnerable to summon-consuming abilities, strong dispels, and spell reflection.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Enchantress:** Enchant takes control of Beastmaster's Boars in lane and turns them against him, while Untouchable slows his Inner Beast attack speed aura.
  - **Chen:** Holy Persuasion permanently steals Beastmaster's Boars, neutral creeps, or dominated units, turning Beastmaster's zoo push into Chen's own army.
  - **Abaddon:** Aphotic Shield removes Primal Roar's stun instantly with a strong dispel and clears Boar poison slows in the laning stage.
  - **Winter Wyvern:** Cold Embrace saves the Primal Roar target from all physical damage dealt by Beastmaster and his high-attack-speed summons, while Winter's Curse turns his Inner Beast aura against him.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Linken's Sphere:** Blocks Primal Roar's instant single-target stun, preventing Beastmaster from initiating from fog with Blink Dagger.
  - **Lotus Orb:** Reflects Primal Roar back onto Beastmaster, stunning and pushing him away for the exact same duration so he cannot follow up on his target.
  - **Crimson Guard:** Damage block heavily mitigates the rapid, low-damage physical attacks from Beastmaster's Boars and summons across the entire team.
  - **Hand of Midas:** Instantly executes Beastmaster's high-bounty Boar or dominated neutral creep, stripping his lane pressure while accelerating your own gold and XP.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**
  - **Monkey King:** Wild Axes clear gigantic swathes of trees on a low cooldown to knock Monkey King off Tree Dance for a 4-second stun, and Hawk provides unobstructed flying vision over tree canopies.
  - **Tinker:** Hawk vision spots Tinker hidden in the treeline after Rearm/Keen Conveyance, Wild Axes clear the trees hiding him, and Primal Roar locks him down through Defense Matrix.
  - **Templar Assassin:** Summon Boar's poison slow and rapid attacks alongside Wild Axes strip Refraction charges quickly and kite Templar Assassin throughout the early and mid game.
  - **Wraith King:** Beastmaster's early zoo timing and Inner Beast attack speed aura overwhelm Wraith King's lane and towers early, while Primal Roar locks him down through Black King Bar.

### Bloodseeker (Agility)
- **Key Weakness:** Bloodseeker relies on Rupture to immobilize targets and Thirst to gain movement and attack speed from low-HP enemies, meaning he struggles against heroes who can stand their ground and manfight him, teleport out, or negate Rupture damage.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Troll Warlord:** When Ruptured, Troll Warlord simply stands still and activates Battle Trance and Whirling Axes, easily out-trading and killing Bloodseeker in a stationary manfight.
  - **Morphling:** Waveform travels without taking Rupture movement damage while invulnerable, and Attribute Shift keeps Morphling at high HP percentages to deny Thirst bonuses.
  - **Medusa:** Mana Shield absorbs incoming damage before health is lost, keeping Medusa near 100% HP for most of the fight so Bloodseeker never gains Thirst speed until she is nearly dead.
  - **Abaddon:** Borrowed Time turns all Rupture movement damage into healing, allowing Abaddon to sprint freely across the map while Ruptured to heal back to full HP.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Town Portal Scroll:** Early in the game, Bloodseeker lacks a built-in stun to cancel TP scrolls, allowing Ruptured targets to simply teleport back to base safely.
  - **Linken's Sphere:** Blocks Rupture from long cast range, preventing Bloodseeker from catching mobile cores in teamfights or split-push lanes.
  - **Lotus Orb:** Reflects Rupture back onto Bloodseeker, forcing him to stand still as well or take severe damage while chasing with high Thirst movement speed.
  - **Eul's Scepter of Divinity / Wind Waker:** Cyclones Bloodseeker during Bloodrage/Thirst chases to waste his buffs, or Wind Waker moves a Ruptured ally without triggering Rupture's distance threshold cleanly.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**
  - **Pangolier:** Rupture completely shuts down Rolling Thunder, as Pangolier's continuous high-speed rolling causes him to take catastrophic pure/HP-removal damage and die if he moves.
  - **Slark:** Thirst passively reveals Slark and disables Essence Shift/Shadow Dance's passive out-of-combat health regeneration whenever Slark drops below the visibility health threshold.
  - **Weaver:** Rupture punishes Weaver's reliance on Shukuchi mobility in fights, forcing Weaver to either stand still and die to Blood Rite silence or trigger Time Lapse immediately.
  - **Anti-Mage:** Rupture prevents Anti-Mage from walking or using short Blinks without taking heavy damage, and Blood Rite's large AoE silence forces out Manta Style before Anti-Mage wants to commit.

### Bounty Hunter (Agility)
- **Key Weakness:** Bounty Hunter has fragile stats in sustained teamfights and relies entirely on Shadow Walk invisibility for scouting and Track for vision and gold, making him easily neutralized by cheap dispels and detection.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Slardar:** Corrosive Haze has a longer duration and lower cooldown than Track, completely countering Shadow Walk while allowing Slardar to run down and bash Bounty Hunter.
  - **Abaddon:** Aphotic Shield dispels Track on allies and absorbs Shuriken Toss burst, while Mist Coil and Borrowed Time deny Track kills.
  - **Legion Commander:** Press the Attack dispels Track while granting health regeneration, and Duel punishes Bounty Hunter whenever he steps close to cast Jinada or Shuriken Toss.
  - **Phantom Lancer:** Doppelganger dispels Track every few seconds, making it nearly impossible for Bounty Hunter to maintain vision on the real Phantom Lancer.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Dust of Appearance / Sentry Wards:** Exposes Bounty Hunter during Shadow Walk and slows him while invisible, turning his scouting attempts into easy kills for the enemy team.
  - **Lotus Orb:** Dispels Track on cast and can reflect Shuriken Toss or Track back onto Bounty Hunter and his teammates.
  - **Manta Style:** Allows agility cores to immediately dispel Track upon activation, denying the enemy team true sight and bonus Track gold.
  - **Eul's Scepter of Divinity:** Dispels Track when cast on yourself and interrupts Bounty Hunter's follow-up initiation.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**
  - **Riki:** Track provides true sight over Riki and grants bonus movement speed to allies, completely negating Cloak and Dagger's permanent invisibility advantage.
  - **Clinkz:** Track exposes Clinkz during Skeleton Walk and allows Bounty Hunter's team to hunt him down whenever he tries to split-push or scout alone.
  - **Sniper:** Bounty Hunter scouts Sniper in the deep backlines with Shadow Walk and marks him with Track, giving gap-closing teammates vision and speed to jump Sniper immediately.
  - **Enigma:** Shadow Walk allows Bounty Hunter to scout Enigma in the jungle early and interrupt Black Hole in teamfights with a tracked Shuriken Toss mini-stun from outside Black Hole's radius.

### Brewmaster (Universal)
- **Key Weakness:** Brewmaster's teamfight impact is heavily concentrated into his Primal Split cooldown and his Drunken Brawler stances, making him vulnerable to instant silences/mana burn before he can split and heroes that rapidly kill his Brewlings.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Death Prophet:** Silence prevents Brewmaster from casting Primal Split after blinking in, and Exorcism spirits deal massive physical damage that quickly destroys his Primal Split Brewlings.
  - **Lion:** Mana Drain and Earth Spike followed by Hex drain Brewmaster's limited mana pool and keep him permanently disabled until he dies without casting Primal Split.
  - **Outworld Destroyer:** Astral Imprisonment and Sanity's Eclipse punish Brewmaster's low intelligence, and Arcane Orb hits his Primal Split Panda units for huge pure damage.
  - **Shadow Shaman:** Hex provides an instant, zero-cast-point disable that locks Brewmaster down before he can press Primal Split, followed by Shackles to finish him off.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Orchid Malevolence / Bloodthorn:** Instant silence stops Brewmaster from activating Primal Split when he drops low, forcing him to buy early defensive dispels instead of aura items.
  - **Black King Bar:** Grants immunity to Cinder Brew ignition, Earth Brewling's Hurl Boulder stun, and Storm Brewling's Cyclone, letting cores ignore the Brewlings in teamfights.
  - **Monkey King Bar:** Pierces the high evasion granted by Brewmaster's Wind Stance in Drunken Brawler, allowing right-clickers to burst him down before he splits.
  - **Diffusal Blade / Disperser:** Burns Brewmaster's tight mana pool so he lacks the mana required to cast Cinder Brew, Thunder Clap, and Primal Split in the same fight.

### Void Spirit (Universal)

  - **Sniper:** Astral Step provides two long-range instant gap-closing charges that allow Void Spirit to jump straight onto Sniper in the backlines, pull him with Aether Remnant, and burst him.
  - **Drow Ranger:** Void Spirit closes the distance instantly with Astral Step to disable Marksmanship, absorbs Gust/Multishot with Resonant Pulse and Dissimilate, and kills her easily.
  - **Zeus:** Astral Step jumps past Heavenly Jump's slow, and Void Spirit's high mobility and burst allow him to hunt Zeus in the backlines before Zeus can output sustained spell damage.

## W

### Warlock (Intelligence)
- **Key Weakness:** Warlock is an immobile backline support with huge cooldowns who becomes largely ineffective if he is jumped and killed before casting Chaotic Offering or if Fatal Bonds is dispelled.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Weaver:** Shukuchi allows Weaver to bypass Chaotic Offering Golems and Upheaval completely to dive straight onto Warlock in the backlines and kill him in seconds.
  - **Oracle:** Fortune's End dispels Fatal Bonds and Shadow Word in an AoE, while Purifying Flames/False Promise saves allies from Chaotic Offering burst.
  - **Silencer:** Global Silence prevents Warlock from dropping Chaotic Offering as a counter-initiation in teamfights, allowing Silencer's team to wipe Warlock's allies first.
  - **Sniper:** Out-ranges Upheaval and Chaotic Offering completely with Take Aim, allowing him to gun down both Warlock and his Golems from safety.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Blink Dagger:** Allows cores to jump directly onto Warlock to cancel Upheaval channeling and kill him before he can cast Fatal Bonds + Chaotic Offering.
  - **Lotus Orb / Manta Style:** Basic dispels remove Fatal Bonds and Shadow Word from yourself or allies, preventing the massive shared teamfight damage amplification.
  - **Diffusal Blade / Disperser:** Slows the Chaotic Offering Golems and Disperser provides an AoE dispel and max-movement speed buff to run straight out of Upheaval.
  - **Black King Bar:** Grants immunity to Upheaval's crippling slow, Fatal Bonds magic/shared damage, and the Flaming Fists / Permanent Immolation of the Golems.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**
  - **Meepo:** Fatal Bonds links all Meepo clones together so that any AoE damage dealt to the pack is multiplied across every single clone, resulting in instant lethal burst.
  - **Chaos Knight:** Fatal Bonds links Chaos Knight with his own Phantasm illusions, causing damage dealt to his illusions to splash directly onto the real Chaos Knight's health bar.
  - **Huskar:** Fatal Bonds and Chaotic Offering Golems overwhelm Huskar in teamfights, while Upheaval's massive AoE slow makes it impossible for Huskar to walk between targets after Life Break.
  - **Enigma:** Chaotic Offering has a huge cast range and AoE stun that pierces Black King Bar, allowing Warlock to stand safely back and immediately cancel Black Hole while spawning Golems on Enigma's head.

### Weaver (Agility)
- **Key Weakness:** Weaver has a tiny base health pool and relies entirely on Shukuchi and Time Lapse to survive; instant stuns, silences, or roots with true sight kill him before he can press his ultimate.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Faceless Void:** Time Dilation freezes Shukuchi cooldown so Weaver cannot go invisible or move fast, and Chronosphere traps him for an easy kill before he can cast Time Lapse.
  - **Bloodseeker:** Rupture punishes Weaver's reliance on high-speed Shukuchi movement—forcing him to stand still or bleed to death—and even if he Time Lapses, Rupture remains active.
  - **Puck:** Waning Rift silences Weaver instantly, and Dream Coil's leash prevents him from casting Time Lapse while leashed and stuns him if he tries to Shukuchi away.

  - **Orchid Malevolence / Bloodthorn:** Instant silence stops Weaver from casting Shukuchi or Time Lapse, forcing him to rush Linken's Sphere, Manta Style, or BKB.

  - **Rod of Atos / Gleipnir:** Roots prevent Weaver from activating Shukuchi or Time Lapse while holding him in place under Dust/Sentry vision.
  - **Dust of Appearance / Gem of True Sight:** Essential for maintaining vision on Weaver during Shukuchi so single-target disables and attacks can connect.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**

  - **Venomancer:** Shukuchi grants unslowable maximum movement speed that completely ignores Venomous Gale and Poison Sting slows, and Time Lapse purges Venomous Gale while restoring all lost HP.

### Windranger (Universal)

### Winter Wyvern (Intelligence)

  - **Linken's Sphere:** Blocks the cast of Winter's Curse on the primary target, preventing Wyvern from turning a grouped teamfight around.
  - **Blink Dagger:** Allows backline jumpers to close the distance on Winter Wyvern during teamfights and burst her before she can cast Cold Embrace or Winter's Curse.
  - **Black King Bar:** Negates the percentage-based magical burn and slow of Arctic Burn as well as the nuke and slow of Splinter Blast.

### Witch Doctor (Intelligence)

## Z

### Zeus (Intelligence)
- **Key Weakness:** Zeus has low mobility (aside from a short Heavenly Jump) and zero physical durability, folding quickly when gap-closing cores reach him with magic immunity or silences.
- **Counter Heroes (Weak Against):**
  - **Anti-Mage:** Counterspell passively reduces all of Zeus's magic damage, Blink closes the gap past Heavenly Jump, and Mana Void turns Zeus's massive mana pool into a team-wiping nuke.
  - **Viper:** Corrosive Skin grants high magic resistance and automatically damages and slows Zeus every time Arc Lightning or Thundergod's Wrath hits Viper anywhere on the map.
  - **Storm Spirit:** Ball Lightning ignores Heavenly Jump's slow and jumps straight onto Zeus in the backlines to lock him down and burst him before he can kite.
  - **Huskar:** Berserker's Blood grants high magic resistance as his HP drops, and Life Break jumps straight onto Zeus to silence/slow him with Inner Fire and melt him.
- **Counter Items:**
  - **Pipe of Insight:** Essential team item whose magic resistance aura and active barrier absorb Thundergod's Wrath and Arc Lightning spam across all five allies.
  - **Black King Bar:** Renders cores completely immune to Arc Lightning, Lightning Bolt, Heavenly Jump, and Thundergod's Wrath, allowing them to run Zeus down unimpeded.
  - **Mage Slayer:** Reduces Zeus's entire spell damage output by a huge percentage if a core or ranged attacker tags him with a single attack.
  - **Eternal Shroud / Glimmer Cape:** Provides massive magic resistance and magic barriers to nullify Zeus's high-frequency magical burst damage.
- **Heroes Countered (Strong Against):**
  - **Terrorblade:** Arc Lightning, Lightning Bolt, and Thundergod's Wrath deal massive magical and max-HP percentage damage that completely bypasses Terrorblade's 40+ armor and shreds his low HP pool and illusions.
  - **Tinker:** Thundergod's Wrath reveals and damages Tinker anywhere on the map—canceling his Blink Dagger in the trees—while Nimbus and Lightning Bolt provide true sight and vision over tree lines.
  - **Bounty Hunter:** Lightning Bolt and Thundergod's Wrath provide instant true sight around their targets, revealing Bounty Hunter during Shadow Walk so he can never scout safely near Zeus.
  - **Riki:** Lightning Bolt grants a large radius of unobstructed vision and true sight on the ground even without a unit target, exposing Riki through Cloak and Dagger while melting his low health pool.
